Meet the watch

Designed around one principle: Nothing unnecessary.

Every surface. Every curve. Every component. Exists for a reason.

Design

Drawn once.
Refined nine hundred times.

Forty-one millimetres of forged steel, held to a tolerance of two microns. The case is not decorated — it is resolved. Every chamfer exists to catch a single line of light, and nothing else.

Explore the marked points on the watch.

Materials

Four substances.
No compromises.

Sapphire Crystal

Clarity without compromise.

A virtually scratch-resistant surface protecting the world beneath it. Double anti-reflective coating removes the glass from the equation entirely.

01

904L Stainless Steel

Denser. Brighter. Harder to work.

A superalloy borrowed from chemical engineering. It resists corrosion, holds a mirror polish, and punishes anyone who machines it carelessly.

02

High-Density Ceramic

Colour that does not age.

Fired at 1,600°C and diamond-polished. The bezel will not scratch, fade, or discolour — not in ten years, not in fifty.

03

Vegetable-Tanned Leather

It begins to record you.

Tanned slowly with tree bark, never chrome. Within a year the strap carries a patina no two owners will ever share.

04

Craft

Eleven weeks.
One watch.

  1. 01Case forged, milled, then finished by hand across nine distinct grains.
  2. 02Movement assembled, disassembled, and assembled again after oiling.
  3. 03Regulated in five positions over fourteen days.
  4. 04Cased, sealed, pressure-tested, then left to run alone for a fortnight.

The Movement

The Heart

Inside the case lies a precision mechanical movement engineered to keep every second accountable. No battery. No circuit. Only a coiled spring, released twenty-eight thousand eight hundred times an hour.
